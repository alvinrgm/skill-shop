# Breakdown template — Phase 2 production document

The breakdown is the Hollywood-studio-style pre-production document for the spot. One table, locks visual + audio + track sources together so audio architecture cannot drift mid-production.

## Why this exists

A `script.md` that mixes visual + dialogue in one column never states the audio architecture, so Phase 5 drifts to Strategy A (per-clip seedance native audio) because that's the cheap path — even when the spot's structure requires Strategy B (continuous narrator). That mismatch only shows up at stringout, and pivoting then costs multiple re-cuts and a pile of wasted credits.

The breakdown forces the audio architecture decision at Phase 2 when it's ~$0 to change. Adding a column for "Narrator VO (11L)" alongside "In-scene dialogue (seedance)" makes the architecture VISIBLE in the table → if rows 1, 4, 6, 7b all have 11L populated, that's a Strategy B spot and we know it upfront.

## The 8-column structure

| # | Sec | Visual | In-scene dialogue (seedance) | Narrator VO (11L) | Music + SFX | Char refs | Env refs |

**Column purposes:**

→ **#** — shot number (1, 2, 3, ..., or sub-cuts like 5a, 5b)
→ **Sec** — target duration in seconds. seedance fast mode has [4, 15] range — keep within. For VO-only ken-burns shots, no seedance minimum.
→ **Visual** — what happens on screen. Camera, characters, action, key props. One paragraph max. `<br><br>` for line breaks in cells.
→ **In-scene dialogue (seedance)** — dialogue lip-synced to on-screen characters, generated by seedance with native audio. Speaker labels in CAPS. Empty `—` if no in-scene dialogue.
→ **Narrator VO (11L)** — voice-over delivered as overlay, not lip-synced. Generated separately (your locked 11L narrator voice) and mixed in. Empty `—` if no VO.
→ **Music + SFX** — what music bed plays, ambient/SFX descriptions in parens. If music is the same continuous bed across all shots, write "continuous Suno bed (see brief)" in row 1 and "(continuous bed)" in subsequent rows.
→ **Char refs** — which character refs (otto_sheet, coworker_extras) feed into this shot. Empty `—` if no characters.
→ **Env refs** — which environment refs (office_sheet, kitchenette) feed in.

## Forcing functions

The columns are designed to surface architecture decisions automatically:

→ **Rule 1 — hybrid audio flag:** If a row has BOTH `In-scene dialogue` AND `Narrator VO` populated → that's a hybrid shot. Plan the overlay timing now (when does the in-scene line land, when does the VO drop in). Don't defer to mix time.
→ **Rule 2 — silent + VO = ken-burns:** If a row has ONLY `Narrator VO` and no in-scene dialogue, and the visual is a static product shot or simple character beat → skip seedance entirely. Use ffmpeg ken-burns over a still + overlay the 11L VO. Saves ~17 credits per shot.
→ **Rule 3 — Strategy B detection:** If multiple rows share the same `Narrator VO` voice (e.g., the 11L narrator on rows 1, 2, 4, 6) → that's a Strategy B spot. Generate the 11L track FIRST as one continuous gen, before any seedance gens. This locks voice consistency across the spot.
→ **Rule 4 — Strategy A detection:** If `Narrator VO` is empty across ALL rows and only `In-scene dialogue` is populated → that's Strategy A. Per-clip seedance native audio is correct. No 11L needed.
→ **Rule 5 — credit-saving audit:** Before any seedance gen fires, scan the breakdown for rows that match Rule 2 (silent + VO). Convert those to ken-burns plan. Surface savings to the operator.

## Voice cast block (after the table)

Below the breakdown table, list each voice with its source-of-truth lock:

```
## Voice cast (lock verbatim per source)

→ NARRATOR (11L) → voice_id `<your locked narrator voice_id>`, model `eleven_v3`, settings `<your tuned stability / similarity_boost / style>`
   Used in shots: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (one word, after Otto's line), 7
   Hushed, reverent wildlife-documentary narrator carrying the through-line.

→ OTTO (seedance) → "late-20s American male, dry mid-pitch voice, flat deadpan delivery, quiet, unhurried."
   Used in shots: 6 (one line only). For a character who recurs across ads, lock an 11L voice instead.

→ COWORKERS → silent extras, no voice.
```

For 11L voices, always log the voice_id. Without it, voice consistency across re-fires is lost.

## Worked example — a wildlife-documentary spoof for an invented insulated-bottle brand

For reference only (the brand, characters and lines are invented). This is the shape a Phase 2 breakdown should take, instead of a split script.md + storyboard.md:

| # | Sec | Visual | In-scene dialogue (seedance) | Narrator VO (11L) | Music + SFX | Char refs | Env refs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | Dawn. Slow push across an empty open-plan office as the overhead lights flicker on row by row; the first workers drift in with lanyards and laptop bags | — | "Dawn, on the fourth floor. The herd begins to gather." | NO music — office room tone, HVAC hum, distant keyboard clicks | coworker_extras | office_sheet (dawn) |
| 2 | 6 | Otto at his desk lifts a sweating plastic cup of lukewarm water, sips, winces, sets it down on a soggy ring of condensation | — | "By ten, his water has gone warm. He drinks it anyway. Survival demands it." | (room tone) | otto_sheet | office_sheet (morning) |
| 3 | 6 | Long-lens tracking shot: Otto weaves between desks to the kitchenette. The water-cooler jug is empty; it gurgles once | — | "The watering hole is a long journey. And today... it has run dry." | (room tone + one hollow gurgle) | otto_sheet, coworker_extras | office_sheet (midday), kitchenette |
| 4 | 8 | Product reveal on a clean desk: steel bottle, ice visible through the open mouth, lid twisting closed. NO CHARACTERS. **→ Rule 2: ken-burns candidate (still + VO overlay, skip seedance)** | — | "Then... a discovery. Double-wall steel. Ice for twenty-four hours. A lid that never leaks into your bag." | orchestral swell enters (Suno, placed in FCP) | — | — (use pack_sheet hero pull) |
| 5 | 5 | 5pm. Otto shakes the bottle; the ice rattles. Across the floor, three coworkers' heads rise above the partitions in unison | — | "Late afternoon. The ice remains. The herd takes notice." | (swell holds, low) + ice rattle | otto_sheet, coworker_extras | office_sheet (late afternoon) |
| 6 | 4 | Otto turns slowly to the long lens, deadpan, aware he is being watched | OTTO (to camera): "Still cold." | "Remarkable." (t=2.5s, after Otto's line) | (room tone) | otto_sheet | office_sheet (late afternoon) |
| 7 | 4 | Clean product end card. Bottle centered on the desk. Logo lockup fades in. **→ Rule 2: ken-burns candidate** | — | "[Brand]. Stay cold in your natural habitat." | (swell resolves → fade) | — | — (still over pack_sheet) |

**Architecture analysis (what the table makes obvious):**
→ 7 rows have the 11L narrator → Strategy B spot. Generate the narrator track FIRST.
→ 1 row has in-scene seedance dialogue → only shot 6 needs a lip-synced line; shots 1, 2, 3, 5 are dialogue-free motion shots.
→ 2 rows (shot 4 + shot 7) are silent + VO only → ken-burns candidates, skip seedance, save ~34 credits.
→ No continuous music bed → every seedance prompt carries the NO MUSIC clause; one Suno orchestral swell is placed in FCP under shots 4-5 and resolves under shot 7.
→ Shot 6 is hybrid → plan overlay timing now (Otto's line at ~0.5-1.5s, narrator's "Remarkable." at 2.5s).

Locking this at Phase 2 is what lets a spot like this ship in v1 instead of v3.

## When to deviate

The breakdown is the default. Deviate only if:
→ Spot is single-shot (1 clip) and very simple → script.md alone is fine
→ Spot is a re-edit of an existing campaign with locked audio → use the prior breakdown as source-of-truth

For everything else, fill the breakdown completely at Phase 2 before any generation fires.

## Naming + storage

Save to `$ADGEN_HOME/<brand>/concepts/<slug>/breakdown.md`. The breakdown is the source of truth for Phases 3-7. Other artifacts (storyboard, character bibles, asset-lock, scene start-frames) reference back to it by shot #.
