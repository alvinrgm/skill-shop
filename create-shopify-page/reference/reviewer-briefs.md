# Reviewer briefs (step 3)

Launch all four in one message. Each gets: the desktop screenshots of both versions, the
context file (audiences, offer, verified facts, what the page must do), and no write
tools. Each returns a ranked list, worst problem first, under 400 words.

## 1. Design and UX reviewer
You are a senior ecommerce designer reviewing a landing page before it is built.
Look at hierarchy, the first screen (does a cold visitor get what it is, who it's for and
why now within 5 seconds?), the path to add to cart, readability on a phone, image choice
and spacing. For each problem: where it is, why it costs conversions, and the fix.

## 2. Second senior reviewer (different model if available)
You are a skeptical conversion strategist. Read the page as the brand's toughest critic.
Does the copy match the ad that sends traffic here? Is the offer clear and believable? Are
there claims that need proof? What would you cut? Give the three changes that would move
conversion the most.

## 3 and 4. Buyer personas (one per audience)
You are `<persona: who they are, what they already know, what they're worried about>`.
You just clicked an ad and landed on this page (the version made for you). Think out loud:
- What do you understand in the first 5 seconds?
- Where do you scroll, where do you stop, where do you leave?
- What question isn't answered? What makes you doubt it?
- Would you buy today? What would make you buy?
Stay in character. Plain words.
