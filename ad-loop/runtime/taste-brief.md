# Taste brief: static-ad polish

A judge calibration input (SKILL.md invariant 1: judges see the verdict-bank README, this
brief and the North-Star picks, nothing else). It encodes what "a human design team made
this" means for a static ad. Replace or extend it with your own operator's verdicts as the
verdict bank grows; keep it short enough that a judge reads all of it.

## The governing read

The bar is **ONE DESIGNED SYSTEM**: layout, colour blocking, type and light conceived
together on one grid. It is not "better AI photography". Failures happen wherever
elements are layered onto an image after the fact. The test: does it look like a human
design team polished and created it?

## What that means in practice

- **Pasted heroes are the commonest tell.** The fix is integration (contact shadow,
  reflected colour, edge light that matches the scene) AND deliberate scale and placement
  on the grid. Cutouts are not banned; floating cutouts are.
- **Scale lies twice.** Generated scenes get physical proportions wrong (a product the
  size of a table), and subjects are placed too small in the canvas. Check both.
- **The image proves the headline.** If the picture could carry a different headline just
  as well, the two are not locked.
- **Voice beats cleverness.** AI-flavoured copy reads as AI-flavoured. A plain declarative
  in the brand's own register (e.g. "we made it simpler. you'll notice.", an invented
  example) beats a pun every time. Write the line a founder would say out loud.
- **One light logic per ad.** Declare it in the concept card. Both halves of any split are
  rendered under it.

## How a batch is killed

Kill, do not fix, on system or proportion failures (design-laws section F). A batch that
fails this brief is rebuilt from scratch under the laws: fewer ads, each fully
designed as one system, delivered only if it would survive next to the North Stars.

## Known limits

- The residual taste gap is often unnameable in the abstract. Operators react to concrete
  work, not questions: the next rebuild is the elicitation instrument.
- Personas judge copy and concept resonance. They cannot judge design craft; this brief and
  the craft instruments do.
